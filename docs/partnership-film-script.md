# Partnership film — shooting script

For the hero frame on `/partnership`, generated in Higgsfield from the Agent
Top Performers Success Day stills.

---

## The brief in one line

A room full of people who all made the same decision, taken seriously by each
other — and seven brand names having a bit of fun over the top of them.

## The idea that makes it work

**The pictures stay slow. The type moves fast.**

Every frame is unhurried, documentary, barely moving — and the brand names
snap on over them: hard, instant, no fades, in their own colours. The contrast
between the calm room and the type flicking about is the whole personality of
the film. Restraint underneath, mischief on top.

## Reference

Drawn from the **massive.work** hero: near-black, unhurried, one subject per
frame, motion so slow you only notice it after a second. Borrow the
**restraint in the imagery** — then break it with the typography, which they
don't do and we will.

*(Working from a still frame of that hero rather than the motion itself.)*

## Specification

| | |
|---|---|
| Runtime | 30 seconds, seamless loop |
| Aspect | 16:9 primary, 4:5 for social |
| Audio | **None** — the hero is muted and autoplaying |
| Delivery | H.264, 1920×1080, ~10–12 MB |
| Grade | **Neutral.** The site applies the vintage treatment (muted room, warm skin, grain, vignette) in CSS. Graded footage would cook twice. |

## Type rules

- **Archivo Extrabold, uppercase, tight tracking** — the same face as the page headline
- Names **snap** on: 2 frames in, no easing, no fade. They can leave on a cut.
- Each name in **its own brand colour**, everything else white
- Names never centre — they sit in a corner, off a shoulder, along an edge
- If a name lands over a face, it lands *beside* it, never across the eyes

## Shot list

### 01 · The room before anyone speaks — 3s
**Source:** the window-blinds frame, blurred foreground, light coming through
**Motion:** very slow push in, almost imperceptible
**Prompt:** *slow dolly in, handheld micro-movement, warm afternoon light through venetian blinds, shallow depth of field, documentary*
**Type:** none — let it be quiet first

### 02 · Someone has the floor — 3s
**Source:** woman in the polka-dot blouse, hands shaping an idea
**Motion:** slow dolly in, settling from her hands to her face
**Prompt:** *slow push in on a woman speaking and gesturing at a boardroom table, natural window light, candid documentary*
**Type:** `THE PROPERTY EXPERTS` snaps into the lower left at 1.5s. Holds. Gone on the cut.

### 03 · The room listens — 2.5s
**Source:** two men at the table, one with a hand at his mouth
**Motion:** locked off, with breath
**Prompt:** *static shot, two men listening intently across a meeting table, minimal movement, shallow focus*
**Type:** `FINE & COUNTRY` snaps top right. No movement — it just *is* there.

### 04 · It isn't all serious — 3s
**Source:** the man in the white polo, leaning back, laughing
**Motion:** gentle push, a touch of drift
**Prompt:** *slow push in on a man laughing and relaxed at a meeting table, warm natural light, candid, film grain*
**Type:** `THE LETTING EXPERTS` lands on the laugh — time it to the exact frame his face breaks

### 05 · Someone stands up — 3s
**Source:** the man in the black tee presenting, hands mid-gesture
**Motion:** slow rise, chest height to eye line
**Prompt:** *slow vertical rise, man standing and presenting to a room, hands gesturing, soft key light*
**Type:** `THE AUCTION COMPANY` rides up the right edge as the camera rises — the one time type moves with the camera

### 06 · THE BURST — 2s
**Source:** hold on the stage presenter with the booklet, projector glow
**Motion:** none. The camera stops dead.
**Prompt:** *static shot, woman presenting on stage holding a document, projector light behind, cinematic*
**Type:** **all seven names flick through, 4 frames each**, same position, centre-left — like someone thumbing a card index. Ends on `THE RECRUITMENT EXPERTS`, which holds a beat longer than the rest.
*This is the fun. Everything before it is setup.*

### 07 · Thinking — 3s
**Source:** the man with glasses pushed up, chin on hand
**Motion:** the slowest push of the film
**Prompt:** *extremely slow push in on a man listening, chin resting on hand, thoughtful, natural light*
**Type:** none. Let the room go quiet again after the burst.

### 08 · Two of them, together — 3s
**Source:** the pair leaning in over the papers
**Motion:** slow drift right
**Prompt:** *slow lateral drift across two colleagues working over documents, warm light, documentary*
**Type:** `THE MORTGAGE EXPERTS` and `THE COMMERCIAL PROPERTY EXPERTS` snap on **together**, stacked, one under the other

### 09 · The whole table — 4.5s
**Source:** the wide of the boardroom — laptops, water glasses, papers
**Motion:** slow pull back, coming to rest
**Prompt:** *slow pull back revealing a full boardroom working together, warm light, documentary wide, settles to a stop*
**Type:** the seven names **stack up one at a time**, 6 frames apart, into a block in the lower third — then all seven cut to white at once and hold for the last second.

**On screen at the end:** this is where **FRIENDS TO YOUR MASSIVE AMBITIONS**
sits on the page, so keep the middle third of frame clear.

## Cutting notes

- **No image cut shorter than 2.5s** — except the burst, which is the exception that proves it
- **Cross-dissolve 6–10 frames** between shots. The pictures dissolve; the type never does.
- Shot 09 must resolve to near-stillness so the loop back to 01 is invisible
- Faces get **held**, not passed over

## What to avoid

- Anyone looking at camera
- Speed ramps, whips, crash zooms, light leaks
- Type that animates in with easing, bounce, or blur — it snaps or it doesn't move
- Lanyards and logos filling frame; the brand is the people
- A hero shot of any one person — nobody here is the star

## Optional: the 60-second social cut

Same footage, sound on, voice-over. Read slowly, long gaps.

> There's a room like this most weeks.
>
> Nobody in it is checking anyone's timesheet.
>
> No one's asking why they left the office at three.
>
> They all run their own business.
>
> They all took the same decision at some point — that they'd rather
> build it themselves than build it for somebody else.
>
> None of them did it alone.
>
> *(beat)*
>
> The Experts Group. Friends to your massive ambitions.

**Music:** one sustained piano or string note under everything. No percussion,
no build. On the burst, cut the music entirely for two seconds — let the type
land in silence, then bring it back.
